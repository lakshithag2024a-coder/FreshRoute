Impact Methodology & Mathematical Model

All platform figures are derived from persistent database records of completed pickups.

Food Waste Avoided (kg) Calculated as: Total Portions Collected × 0.50 kg/portion. Based on standard institutional catering portion weights.
CO2e Emissions Prevented (kg) Calculated as: Food Waste Avoided (kg) × 2.50 kg CO2e/kg. Based on FAO Food Wastage Footprint lifecycle analysis model.
Rescue Efficiency Rate (%) Calculated as: Successful Pickups / (Successful Pickups + Expired) × 100. Measures community claim responsiveness.
