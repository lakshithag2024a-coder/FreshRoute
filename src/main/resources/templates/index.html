How FreshRoute Rescues Surplus Food

A closed-loop system designed to make food redistribution effortless for kitchens and community organizers.

1. Donors Post Surplus

Kitchens and stores list surplus batches with portion counts, pickup windows, allergen details, and a verified food safety pledge.

2. Smart Matching & Reserve

Our rule-based engine scores listings by urgency, distance, and capacity fit so recipients reserve exact needs in a click.

3. Pickup & Verification

Recipients pick up food using unique verification codes. Donors confirm handover, instantly logging saved meals and CO2 savings.

FreshRoute Food Safety & Compliance Framework

FreshRoute serves as a logistics and discovery platform connecting surplus food donors with authorized community recipients. All donors warrant compliance with local health regulations and appropriate temperature control. Recipients inspect items prior to consumption.
